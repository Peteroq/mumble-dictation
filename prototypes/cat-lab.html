Cat Lab

A flat field, not a model: one silhouette, an edge that glows, and grain the edge dissolves into.

Grain

0.26

Deliberately past what film would do. Applied before the tone map, so it lives in the picture rather than on top of it.

1.6

Pixels per grain. Above about 3 it stops reading as grain and starts reading as a dither pattern, which is its own look.

0.70

The grain eating into the coverage instead of sitting on the colour. This is what breaks the outline into specks rather than fading it as a clean gradient — the whole point of the look.

0.20

How deep into the body the dissolve bites. Deep enough and the cat is made of specks; shallow and only its outline is.

10

New grain per second. Zero freezes it — still grain, no shimmer. Around 10 is film; 60 is television static.
